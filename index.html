What goes into every project

A lot of people see a 3D render or VR tour and think it's just a video. It isn't. Every project we deliver is built entirely from scratch — every wall, every surface, every light source is individually crafted.

Step 01
Blueprint Study
We study every dimension, wall, opening, and floor plan in detail before touching the software.
Step 02
3D Modeling
Every single wall, column, window, door, staircase, and surface is built by hand in 3D — vertex by vertex.
Step 03
Materials & Textures
Marble, wood, concrete, glass — each material is applied and tuned to look exactly like the real thing.
Step 04
Lighting Setup
Sunlight, shadows, interior lamps — realistic lighting that changes how the entire space feels.
Step 05
VR Programming
For interactive projects, every door hinge, light switch, and lift button is individually coded and tested.
Step 06
Render & Export
Final rendering can take hours of processing time per scene to achieve photorealistic quality.
The honest truth: what you receive in 7 days represents 50–100+ hours of skilled technical work. A professional 3D studio in Delhi or Mumbai charges ₹3–5 lakh for the same output. Our pricing reflects our genuine effort — not a shortcut.
